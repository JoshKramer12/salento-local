## Failure response — one operator decision at a time

| Observation | Immediate action | Follow-up requiring separate approval |
|---|---|---|
| Card decline, authentication failure or Stripe rejection | Keep or close the gate; confirm no succeeded charge and no paid membership/commission. | A new card attempt is a **new** charge authorization; investigate account or method first. |
| Timeout, ambiguous browser result, signed webhook lag or PaymentIntent succeeded without local PAID | Close new creation, preserve the provider ID/event, inspect Stripe status and webhook delivery, then reconcile the exact existing Payment. | Manual replay/recovery only after proving idempotency and exact provider identity; no second charge or manufactured PAID row. |
| TEST Customer/PaymentIntent/Subscription ID appears or account/mode differs | Stop, close the gate, preserve evidence and verify key/account/metadata. | No relabeling or silent ID replacement. Correct config/data only under a reviewed plan. |
| Wrong €100 amount/currency, Owner, Partner, business, term or agreement | Stop, close the gate and compare provider charge to local record. | Any refund, correction or second payment needs explicit financial-owner approval. |
| Membership absent or duplicate, commission absent/duplicated, 14-day hold wrong, receipt or notification failed | Stop further payments; retain signed event and queue state, reconcile exact IDs and logs. | Fix or authorized repair after independent review. Do not make a payout or rely on an email retry during the paused cron. |
| Unexpected subscription, renewal, transfer, payout or Cash Out gate | Close payment creation and any accidentally enabled request/send gate; preserve webhook intake and provider state. | Provider cancellation, payout reversal or refund is a separate approved financial action. |
| Refund genuinely required | Preserve payment and commission linkage; use the approved refund runbook and actual Stripe object evidence. | LIVE owner self-service refund gate remains off; no automatic real refund or blind retry. |

An application rollback does not reverse the card charge, membership/commission entries or a Stripe transaction. Do not restore the prepayment database over new provider history. A future €50 Cash Out requires its own LIVE Connect, funds, destination, monitoring, 14-day-hold and operator approval gates.
