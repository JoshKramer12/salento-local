## Payment-path and data-transition audit

Owner creation, Partner-originated submissions, Owner handoff, Pay Later, additional listings and claims converge on `BusinessSubmission` and the initial payment finalizer. The card Payment Element creates a server-priced €100 PaymentIntent; optional recurring setup creates a Customer/Subscription/invoice; card Checkout and bank transfer are other paths. A signed platform webhook or verified provider read finalizes a payment, publishes/extends the business membership, and associates Partner attribution. The webhook checks signature and mode and logs a unique event ID; payment/provider IDs and commission `sourceKey` enforce replay idempotency. Initial commissions are €50 with a 14-day paid-at hold; renewal/reactivation commissions remain €15. Refund and dispute paths reverse or create debt. The Owner self-service live-refund gate is separate and remains off.

Stage 10A/10C already protected pending Checkout, bank and embedded subscription objects and stored automatic-renewal recovery. Stage 10E found and fixed two remaining LIVE-mode holes locally: manual renewal could reuse an old customer/pending session or bank intent, and the manual initial Payment Element could retrieve an untagged historical intent. New manual intents and renewal sessions now record mode; initial/renewal finalizers retain the verified customer ID on the paid row. A production LIVE canary now requires `LIVE_PAYMENT_SCOPE=canary` and one named submission ID, permits only a manual **card** creation for that ID, blocks bank/recurring/renewal creation and real Partner disbursement, and makes the existing membership cron return a paused result during the canary. Closing the creation gate still permits verified signed LIVE settlement. None of these changes has been deployed.

Historical TEST/NULL Payment and commission rows remain intact. The live Cash Out eligibility query requires an explicitly LIVE paid Payment, the 14-day initial hold, no unresolved refund and the existing reservation checks; historical TEST/NULL commissions therefore cannot fund a real withdrawal. Listing and login relationships do not depend on Stripe key mode. A **short, named first canary can proceed without rewriting historical records** once the code and operational gates above are approved: it uses a fresh submission without provider references, all other payment creation is blocked, the cron avoids TEST retries, and Cash Out stays off. A broad public LIVE launch cannot safely ignore the 12 active TEST subscriptions, the failed lookup, pending test objects, historical reminders and customer communication. Preserve records, separately review/cancel or segregate TEST provider subscriptions with owner consent, retain an audit mapping, then reconcile local automatic-renewal state. Do not repurpose TEST IDs or silently reclassify NULL rows.

The existing cron currently retries outcome, failure, commission, purchase and bank-instruction deliveries; it also sends daily membership and Pay Later reminders. During the proposed LIVE canary its new early return prevents old TEST/unknown jobs from reading LIVE Stripe or sending misleading mail. Immediate signed-webhook delivery attempts still run; if any canary confirmation fails, stop and resolve it manually before another payment. Before general LIVE service, implement or approve a provenance-aware queue transition and resume the cron only after its pending obligations are reviewed. Delayed TEST webhooks will fail the LIVE mode check; do not interpret their rejection as a LIVE payment failure.
