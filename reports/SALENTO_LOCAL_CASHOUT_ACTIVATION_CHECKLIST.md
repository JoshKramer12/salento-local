## Emergency stop and rollback

Pause new requests first, then the send worker; keep signed webhooks, reconciliation and read-only health scans operating for in-flight payouts. Preserve `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` and `ACTION_REQUIRED` reservations. Do not retry a Stripe create after an unknown outcome merely because an HTTP request failed. Confirm provider objects, idempotency keys, balances and webhook history before any operator reversal or retry. A deployment rollback requires checking compatibility with already-applied migrations and in-flight events; a database restore requires separate authorization and replay/reconciliation of provider events after the backup timestamp.
