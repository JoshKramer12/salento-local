# Salento Local — proposed Stage 10B activation plan

**Plan only, 19 November 2026. No step is authorized by this document.** The present production site uses test Stripe keys and all Cash Out send gates are off. Every deployment, live credential/configuration change, real payment, onboarding and payout requires its own explicit approval and recorded operator, time, SHA, account and evidence. The financial owner sets exposure and reserve values; none are inferred from test balances.

## Recovery and exception boundaries

Emergency stop is requests off, then processor off; preserve signed webhooks, read-only inventory and authorized reconciliation of in-flight events. Code rollback cannot reverse a Stripe transfer/payout; a DB restore can erase knowledge of provider effects, so hold sends until full post-backup provider replay. The provider-backed `payout.failed`, `payout.canceled` and late-return scenarios remain unavailable in the present €46.85 test sandbox. A financial owner may propose a documented exception **only** after supported test funding routes are exhausted, mock/signed-replay/genuine evidence is separated, monitoring and manual canary review are proven, and the user approves that residual risk explicitly. No exception changes the €50 app minimum or authorizes real money by implication.
